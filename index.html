Common questions

Is it free?

Yes. Make an account and play every program the game unlocks for you.

Is this an official TESDA assessment?

No. It is a TESDA-style practice simulator for the Computer Systems Servicing COC 1–4 tasks (PC assembly, networks, servers and repair), built to help you rehearse the procedures.

Why do I need an account?

So each person has their own XP, rank, achievements and completed modules. Two people on one device never share progress.

Where is my progress stored?

In this browser, under your username. Clearing site data or switching devices starts a fresh account.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. The game is built for touch, and you can go fullscreen from the play page.

Can I move my progress to another device?

Yes. On the home screen of the game, press Copy my progress code. On the other device, paste it into Restore. The code holds your progress, not a password.

Can my trainer see my progress?

Only if you send it. Press Copy trainer summary in the game and send the code to your trainer. Trainers paste the codes into the For trainers page. Nothing is uploaded.
